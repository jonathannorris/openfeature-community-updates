# OpenFeature Community Update: Sep 10 to Sep 24, 2026

_A biweekly digest of the most notable pull requests, issues, and discussions across the [open-feature](https://github.com/open-feature) org._

## TL;DR

- **Provider conformance is moving toward shared evidence:** [spec#417](https://github.com/open-feature/spec/issues/417) tracks the cross-language suite, while [spec#424](https://github.com/open-feature/spec/issues/424) works through report collection, trust, and expiry.
- **flagd readiness and sync health are more dependable and observable:** [flagd#2050](https://github.com/open-feature/flagd/pull/2050) waits for applied state, and [flagd#2051](https://github.com/open-feature/flagd/pull/2051) adds freshness and reconnect metrics.
- **Dart's server v0.9 work has landed major capability and hook slices:** [dart-sdk#171](https://github.com/open-feature/dart-sdk/pull/171) and [dart-sdk#172](https://github.com/open-feature/dart-sdk/pull/172) are merged, with typed events and domain isolation in [dart-sdk#182](https://github.com/open-feature/dart-sdk/pull/182).
- **Hook and provider lifecycle behavior is advancing across SDKs:** [cpp-sdk#136](https://github.com/open-feature/cpp-sdk/pull/136), [php-sdk#187](https://github.com/open-feature/php-sdk/pull/187), and [js-sdk-contrib#1631](https://github.com/open-feature/js-sdk-contrib/pull/1631) cover execution, lifecycle, and transport safety.
- **The provider ecosystem continues to broaden:** [openfeature.dev#1478](https://github.com/open-feature/openfeature.dev/pull/1478) adds a Flagsmith Swift provider, and [openfeature.dev#1491](https://github.com/open-feature/openfeature.dev/pull/1491) adds Featureflip's PHP provider.

## Spec & Protocol
### Proposals to watch

- **An official OpenFeature skill is proposed:** [spec#436](https://github.com/open-feature/spec/issues/436) would give AI coding agents a language-neutral guide to providers, clients, evaluation context, hooks, events, tracking, and common lifecycle mistakes.

## Provider Conformance

- **The cross-language provider test suite has a clearer publication track:** [spec#417](https://github.com/open-feature/spec/issues/417) tracks shared Gherkin scenarios, a control API, and a canonical flag set across language implementations. **Reporting is the next design layer:** [spec#424](https://github.com/open-feature/spec/issues/424) focuses on collecting machine-readable results from hosted and external providers, and on how reports should establish trust and expiry.

## flagd & Operator
### flagd

- **Readiness now waits for usable configuration:** [flagd#2050](https://github.com/open-feature/flagd/pull/2050) keeps `/readyz` unavailable until every configured sync source has applied state, closing the gap where readiness could precede evaluable flags.
- **Sync freshness and connection health are now measurable:** [flagd#2051](https://github.com/open-feature/flagd/pull/2051) adds applied-configuration timestamps, active stream and reconnect metrics, and common provider, URI, and selector attributes while retaining legacy keys for dashboard migration.
- **Flagd providers are gaining configurable transport behavior:** [flagd#2045](https://github.com/open-feature/flagd/pull/2045) makes file polling configurable, while [go-sdk-contrib#949](https://github.com/open-feature/go-sdk-contrib/pull/949) adds stream, keep-alive, and offline-poll options and moves evaluation v2 to gRPC on the wire.
- **The deprecated sync metadata RPC is on the removal path:** [flagd#2069](https://github.com/open-feature/flagd/issues/2069) tracks deleting `sync.GetMetadata` in favor of `sync_context`, a breaking protocol cleanup.

### Operator

- **A pod injection bug is blocking updates and deletion:** [open-feature-operator#860](https://github.com/open-feature/open-feature-operator/issues/860) reports that the UPDATE webhook re-injects the flagd sidecar, causing Kubernetes to reject immutable container changes and leaving foreground deletions stuck.

## SDKs & Providers

- **Dart's v0.9 server track is becoming capability-oriented:** [dart-sdk#171](https://github.com/open-feature/dart-sdk/pull/171) makes initialization, shutdown, hooks, and tracking opt-in provider capabilities, while [dart-sdk#172](https://github.com/open-feature/dart-sdk/pull/172) adds evaluation options and typed hook contracts. [dart-sdk#177](https://github.com/open-feature/dart-sdk/pull/177) hardens context snapshots, and [dart-sdk#182](https://github.com/open-feature/dart-sdk/pull/182) proposes typed provider events routed by provider and domain.
- **SDK boundaries are protecting evaluation state:** [go-sdk#601](https://github.com/open-feature/go-sdk/pull/601) copies provider metadata before exposing it to hooks and applications, preventing downstream mutation of provider-owned state.
- **C++ now has the complete hook execution pipeline:** [cpp-sdk#134](https://github.com/open-feature/cpp-sdk/pull/134) separates global hook management into a thread-safe manager, and [cpp-sdk#136](https://github.com/open-feature/cpp-sdk/pull/136) implements before, after, error, and finally stages.
- **PHP lifecycle support is in flight:** [php-sdk#180](https://github.com/open-feature/php-sdk/pull/180) exposes evaluation and resolution metadata, while [php-sdk#187](https://github.com/open-feature/php-sdk/pull/187) adds opt-in initialization and shutdown, provider states, events, recovery, and safe provider replacement.
- **The JavaScript flagd provider is addressing secure, replaceable transports:** [js-sdk-contrib#1630](https://github.com/open-feature/js-sdk-contrib/pull/1630) accepts caller-owned gRPC channel credentials for mTLS and rotation policies, while [js-sdk-contrib#1631](https://github.com/open-feature/js-sdk-contrib/pull/1631) prevents invalid initial payloads from reporting readiness and cancels reconnect work after disconnect.
- **A client-side Flipt provider is proposed for Go:** [go-sdk-contrib#922](https://github.com/open-feature/go-sdk-contrib/issues/922) would evaluate from a locally refreshed snapshot instead of making a network request on every evaluation.
